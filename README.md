## 1. Project Overview

This repository contains the computational workflow, analytical procedures, datasets, intermediate outputs, and visualisations developed for a bibliometric investigation of the relationship between **energy governance and spatial dimensions of energy research**.

The study investigates whether governance-oriented scholarship and spatially oriented scholarship have developed as integrated knowledge domains or whether they remain conceptually fragmented.

Rather than treating governance and spatial research as independent thematic categories, the project examines their **intersection, intellectual structure, thematic maturity, conceptual relationships, and structural gaps**.

The central analytical proposition is:

> **Energy governance and spatial research may individually demonstrate substantial scholarly development while remaining insufficiently integrated at the conceptual level.**

The project therefore moves beyond conventional publication and citation statistics to investigate **what the literature connects, what it isolates, and what it systematically fails to connect**.

---

# 2. Research Objectives

The project has six principal objectives:

1. **Characterise the evolution and intellectual structure of the research corpus.**
2. **Identify the governance and spatial dimensions represented in the literature.**
3. **Measure the degree of governance–spatial overlap within the corpus.**
4. **Identify dominant governance–spatial conceptual relationships.**
5. **Evaluate thematic maturity, centrality, density, and strategic positioning.**
6. **Identify structural knowledge gaps through governance–spatial co-absence analysis.**

---

# 3. Core Research Questions

The computational workflow addresses the following questions:

### RQ1 — Intellectual Development
How has the governance–spatial energy literature evolved over time?

### RQ2 — Intellectual Structure
What are the dominant concepts, authors, institutions, countries, and thematic communities within the corpus?

### RQ3 — Governance–Spatial Integration
To what extent are governance and spatial concepts jointly represented within the literature?

### RQ4 — Conceptual Relationships
Which governance concepts are most strongly associated with spatial concepts?

### RQ5 — Thematic Maturity
Which research themes are motor, basic/transversal, emerging/declining, or highly developed/isolated?

### RQ6 — Knowledge Gaps
Which theoretically possible governance–spatial relationships remain weakly represented or absent?
